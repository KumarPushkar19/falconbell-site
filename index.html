How we work

Three steps, so both sides build trust before volumes grow.

  1. STEP 1

    Sample

    Tell us your specification. We courier a sample for your own testing.

  2. STEP 2

    Trial order

    A small first order, usually by air, to check quality, documents and delivery end to end.

  3. STEP 3

    Regular supply

    Commercial lots by sea on an agreed schedule, to the same specification each time.
